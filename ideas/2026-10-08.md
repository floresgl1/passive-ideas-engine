# Daily Ideas — 2026-10-08

### 1. Forecast Calibration Ledger — [Passive]
- competence: unlabeled
- labeled_at: —

**Idea:** A small hosted app and API where forecasters, founders, and traders log probability calls, mark them resolved, and get Brier scores, reliability curves, and an explicit "too few resolved calls to tell" verdict instead of false precision.
**Leverages:** Applied statistical reasoning (CI-vs-breakeven gating, sample-size intuition) from finance_bot, plus REST API design and relational data modeling from stripe-reconciler and ledger-core.
**One new thing to learn:** Proper scoring rules (Brier/log score) and reliability-diagram binning with small-sample uncertainty.
**Why it's worth it:** Calibration tracking is a real, recurring need with a thin set of polished tools, and a subscription fits it well. The honest-uncertainty framing is the differentiator, and it runs unattended once built.

### 2. Live-Trade Edge Verdict — [Leverage]
- competence: unlabeled
- labeled_at: —

**Idea:** Upload a broker trade-history CSV and get a one-page verdict on whether the realized win rate and payoff ratio clear the breakeven line at a confidence-interval lower bound, or whether the sample is too small to say.
**Leverages:** finance_bot's CI-lower-bound vs. dynamic-breakeven gate and realized P&L attribution, plus document-ingestion work from voice-quiz and studyprof.
**One new thing to learn:** Normalizing the CSV export formats of several retail brokers into one trade schema.
**Why it's worth it:** Retail traders rarely test their own edge honestly. A pay-per-report tool reuses an already-shipped statistical gate, and it applies to live trades, which is not what the existing backtest-audit ideas cover.
