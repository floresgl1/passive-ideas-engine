# Daily Ideas — 2026-11-12
### 1. Deferred Revenue Schedule Builder — [Passive]
- competence: unlabeled
- labeled_at: —

**Idea:** A small paid tool that reads a SaaS company's Stripe subscription invoices and produces the month-by-month deferred-revenue schedule and balanced journal entries (annual plans, mid-cycle upgrades, refunds), ready to import into QuickBooks or Xero.
**Leverages:** The integer-cents, zero-sum double-entry discipline shipped in stripe-reconciler and ledger-tieout, plus the multi-currency and Xero/QuickBooks export work and the Stripe OAuth connection flow.
**One new thing to learn:** Revenue-recognition rules for subscriptions (straight-line recognition over the service period, and how proration, upgrades and refunds re-shape the schedule).
**Why it's worth it:** Any SaaS selling annual plans books cash up front and has to spread it across twelve months, and small teams and their bookkeepers do this by hand in spreadsheets every month-end; it is a recurring, well-defined job that sells as a subscription and sits beside payout reconciliation rather than overlapping it.

### 2. Walkthrough Voice-to-Report — [Experience]
- competence: unlabeled
- labeled_at: —

**Idea:** A phone web app where a property manager or home inspector talks through a walkthrough room by room and gets back a structured, itemized PDF condition report (room, item, condition, action needed), sold as a flat monthly subscription.
**Leverages:** voice-quiz's recorded-audio-to-Whisper transcription path, Claude-generated constrained JSON from free text, and its PDF export; the bounded, enumerated-output habits from finance_bot's agents keep the extraction from inventing items.
**One new thing to learn:** Building an installable, offline-tolerant phone web app that queues audio recordings and uploads them when signal returns.
**Why it's worth it:** Move-in/move-out and inspection reports are typed up after the fact from memory and photos, which is slow and disputed; this hardens the voice pipeline against real field audio and real paying users, which the quiz app never had, in a domain unrelated to the current build.

Profile is unchanged since the last refresh (only the date moved) and direct extractions are tapped, so these are two invention-mode ideas rather than a full set.
