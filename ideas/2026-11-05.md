# Daily Ideas — 2026-11-05
### 1. LLM-Judge Calibration Kit — [Passive]
- competence: unlabeled
- labeled_at: —

**Idea:** A pip-installable CLI (sold with a license key) that takes a team's LLM-as-judge prompt plus 50–200 human-labeled examples and reports whether the judge can be trusted: agreement with confidence intervals, leniency/strictness bias, and which categories of answer it misgrades.
**Leverages:** voice-quiz's Claude-based grading with transcription-artifact leniency, plus the CI-lower-bound and small-sample reasoning from finance_bot and profit_engine_research.
**One new thing to learn:** Inter-rater agreement statistics (Cohen's kappa, bootstrap intervals on agreement) applied to LLM graders.
**Why it's worth it:** Teams shipping LLM graders have a real, current pain: nobody checks the judge itself. A small offline tool with no hosted infrastructure to maintain, and it is a different product from the existing benchmark and drift-canary ideas.

Profile is stale and extractions are tapped, so only one invention-mode idea today rather than padding.
