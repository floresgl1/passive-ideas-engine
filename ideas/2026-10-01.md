### 1. Prompt Drift Canary — [Passive]
- competence: unlabeled
- labeled_at: —

**Idea:** A scheduled GitHub Action (plus hosted dashboard) that replays a team's small "golden prompt" set against their production LLM model on a cron, scores outputs with an LLM judge, and alerts only when the score shift exceeds measured run-to-run noise — catching silent provider model updates that quietly break prompts.
**Leverages:** LLM-as-judge structured grading [emerging] (voice-quiz), CI/CD and scheduled automation [strong] (cron workflows), applied statistical reasoning [strong] (CI bounds vs. a computed baseline) and transition-only alerting from production observability [strong] (finance_bot).
**One new thing to learn:** estimating a judge's own noise floor (repeat-run variance plus agreement against a small human-labeled set) so "drift" alerts mean something rather than firing on sampling jitter.
**Why it's worth it:** every team shipping an LLM feature has this blind spot and most only notice via user complaints; it is a new product (not an extraction from any repo), sells as a flat monthly subscription with near-zero upkeep once running, and doesn't overlap the active ops-triage template build.
