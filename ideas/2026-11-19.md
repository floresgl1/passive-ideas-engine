# Daily Ideas — 2026-11-19
### 1. Donor Receipts and Restricted-Fund Books for Small Nonprofits — [Passive]
- competence: unlabeled
- labeled_at: —

**Idea:** A subscription service that connects to a small nonprofit's Stripe account, books every donation into fund-accounting journal entries (unrestricted vs. restricted by campaign, with fees split out), sends gifts that have no clear designation to a review list instead of guessing a fund, and produces the IRS-required written acknowledgments for gifts of $250 or more plus a year-end giving statement for each donor.
**Leverages:** stripe-reconciler's per-connection Stripe OAuth, payout-to-journal booking, and explicit Suspense account; ledger-tieout's processor-agnostic zero-sum journal and `REVIEW_*` flagging; integer cents and the QuickBooks/Xero export path already shipped for multi-currency.
**One new thing to learn:** Nonprofit fund accounting: tracking restricted net assets and booking the "release from restriction" entry when a restricted gift is spent.
**Why it's worth it:** Thousands of small charities, PTAs, and clubs take donations through Stripe and have a volunteer treasurer doing restricted funds and donor letters by hand in a spreadsheet every January. Once it's connected it runs on its own every payout and every year-end, it uses the deepest ledger skills in the profile on a new customer, and it doesn't overlap with the current build.

### 2. Home-Exercise Rep and Range-of-Motion Log — [Leverage]
- competence: unlabeled
- labeled_at: —

**Idea:** A phone app that a physical-therapy patient props up while doing their assigned home exercises; it counts reps, measures the joint angle reached on each rep (knee bend after surgery, shoulder raise), and produces a weekly one-page PDF the patient can share with their therapist. It reports measured angles, not a diagnosis or a verdict on how well they're doing.
**Leverages:** golf-swing-analyzer's single-phone-video pose pipeline (MediaPipe/ML Kit, scale-normalized body angles, the confidence-based pose-quality gate that rejects frames with poor landmark detection), the Flutter on-device port with share-sheet export, and the project's habit of separating a measurement from a verdict.
**One new thing to learn:** Splitting a continuous joint-angle signal into individual reps (peak/valley detection with hysteresis) so a pause or a wobble isn't counted as an extra rep.
**Why it's worth it:** Therapists rarely know whether patients actually do their home programs or how far range of motion is coming back between visits, and patients have no record to show them. Everything runs on the phone, so a one-time unlock has no server cost, and the golf pipeline carries over almost unchanged to a market that isn't sport.

Profile is unchanged since the last refresh, so these are two invention-mode ideas rather than a full set.
