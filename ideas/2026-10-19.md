# Daily Ideas — 2026-10-19

### 1. Prediction-Market Data Lint — [Passive]
- competence: unlabeled
- labeled_at: —

**Idea:** A pip-installable CLI plus hosted checker where people upload Kalshi/Polymarket candle or trade exports and get a report flagging fake edges: crossed-quote "arbitrage", midpoint prices from empty books, and decision times that look ahead through a close price.
**Leverages:** profit_engine_research's read-only venue adapters and the research screens that rejected a fake 84c arbitrage, look-ahead via close-based decision time, and empty-book midpoints.
**One new thing to learn:** Packaging research checks as a versioned, documented rule set with stable output schema and per-seat licensing (Gumroad/Stripe) for outside users.
**Why it's worth it:** Every prediction-market backtester hits these traps, the checks already exist and are tested, and a lint tool sells repeatedly with almost no support load.

### 2. Queue-Position Fill Simulator Library — [Leverage]
- competence: unlabeled
- labeled_at: —

**Idea:** A small zero-dependency Python library that takes a public trade tape and a resting order's queue position and returns honest paper fills, with fees that block trading when unknown instead of defaulting to zero.
**Leverages:** profit_engine_research's `maker/` queue-position fill model, frozen-dataclass book types, and fee schedule handling.
**One new thing to learn:** Generalizing a venue-specific fill model into a venue-agnostic public API, published to PyPI as ledger-core was.
**Why it's worth it:** It is fast to ship from tested code, reuses the PyPI release path already proven, and gives bot builders an honest alternative to optimistic fill assumptions.
