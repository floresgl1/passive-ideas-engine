# Daily Ideas — 2026-10-12

### 1. Receipt Inbox for Freelancer Books — [Passive]
- competence: unlabeled
- labeled_at: —

**Idea:** Forward a receipt or invoice PDF/photo to a dedicated address and get it parsed by an LLM into a balanced double-entry journal line, with anything the model is unsure about parked in an explicit Suspense account and a monthly CSV export for the user's accountant.
**Leverages:** Document ingestion for LLM pipelines, LLM structured-output generation, double-entry data modeling (integer cents, Suspense over silent defaults), and the web security work on tokens and tenant isolation.
**One new thing to learn:** Receiving inbound email (an inbound-parse webhook) and handling attachments safely.
**Why it's worth it:** Solo freelancers pay a small monthly fee to stop shoebox bookkeeping, and the "uncertain goes to Suspense" rule is a real trust differentiator over tools that guess silently.

### 2. Datasheet Pin-and-Limit Extractor — [Leverage]
- competence: unlabeled
- labeled_at: —

**Idea:** Upload a component datasheet PDF and get back a structured JSON/CSV of pinout, absolute-maximum ratings, and recommended operating ranges, each value tagged with its source page and a "not found" marker rather than a guess.
**Leverages:** PDF text extraction, constrained-JSON LLM extraction, and circuit-analysis fluency, which together make it possible to sanity-check extracted limits.
**One new thing to learn:** Extracting tables from PDFs with messy layouts (table-aware parsing beyond plain text).
**Why it's worth it:** Hobbyist and small-hardware makers re-read datasheets constantly, and a paid per-document API or credit pack sells repeatedly with little upkeep.

Extraction material is thin until the profile refreshes, so today is two invention ideas rather than four.
