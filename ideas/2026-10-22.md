# Daily Ideas — 2026-10-22

### 1. Dispute Evidence Packager — [Passive]
- competence: unlabeled
- labeled_at: —

**Idea:** A hosted service that listens for a Stripe dispute webhook, pulls the matching charge, receipts, delivery and usage records into a single evidence packet, and has an LLM draft the response with every claim cited to a source record and any missing evidence listed as a gap rather than invented.
**Leverages:** stripe-reconciler (webhook signature verification, tenant isolation, integer-cents ledger), voice-quiz (constrained-JSON LLM generation, whitelist sanitization), and finance_bot's fail-closed halt-on-ambiguity habits.
**One new thing to learn:** Stripe's Disputes API and card-network evidence-field requirements (submitting evidence and respecting response deadlines).
**Why it's worth it:** Small merchants lose disputes mostly for lack of organized evidence, the pain recurs on every chargeback, and a flat monthly fee or per-dispute price runs without ongoing effort once built.
