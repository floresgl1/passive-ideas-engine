### 1. Solver-Verified Circuit Drill Deck — [Passive]
- competence: unlabeled
- labeled_at: —

**Idea:** A sold-once, bring-your-own-key voice drill app for circuit-analysis exam prep (FE Electrical, intro circuits courses) whose problems are generated with randomized values and answered by an MNA solver, so every answer key is correct by construction rather than LLM-guessed.
**Leverages:** circuit-simulator's MNA solver and netlist reader, voice-quiz's spoken question/answer loop with LLM-as-judge grading, and its whitelist SVG sanitizer for diagrams.
**One new thing to learn:** Parameterized problem templates (randomizing component values while keeping the circuit well-posed and answers non-degenerate) plus packaging and selling a static study product.
**Why it's worth it:** Students and exam candidates pay for practice sets, and wrong answer keys are the known failure of LLM-generated problems; a solver-checked key is a concrete, demonstrable edge that needs almost no upkeep once shipped.
